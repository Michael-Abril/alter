## 🎯 8 Key Business Structure Decisions - FINALIZED

### 1. Creator Revenue Split Model
**DECISION: Tiered Performance Split (40-60% creator share)**
- **Starter Tier**: 40% creator, 60% company (first 30 days or $5K revenue)
- **Growth Tier**: 50/50 split ($5K-25K revenue)  
- **Premium Tier**: 60% creator, 40% company ($25K+ revenue)

**Rationale**: This protects company margins during the validation phase while incentivizing long-term creator partnerships. Based on your unit economics analysis showing $8-9 profit margins at 50/50, this structure ensures sustainability.

### 2. Creator Transparency Level
**DECISION: Hybrid Transparency Model**
- Creators know they're part of a network but maintain "exclusive product" messaging
- Clear communication: "You're our exclusive partner for [Product X] in your niche"
- Backend reality: Same product can run in different niches simultaneously
- Prevents "dropship discovery" issues while maintaining creator buy-in

### 3. Product Sourcing Strategy
**DECISION: Proven Winner Replication + Creator Input**
- Start with 10-15 validated products from successful dropship campaigns
- Use AliExpress/supplier network you've identified
- Add creator feedback loop for product requests
- Focus on 2-3x markup products with >65% gross margin before split

### 4. Niche Selection
**DECISION: Multi-Niche Launch (3 verticals)**
**Primary Launch Niches:**
- **Home & Organization** (trending, evergreen demand)
- **Pet Products** (passionate audience, high engagement)
- **Wellness/Self-Care** (growing market, premium pricing potential)

**Selection Criteria**: 5K-50K follower creators, high engagement rates, proven purchasing behavior

### 5. Store Infrastructure
**DECISION: Shopify + Custom Landing Pages**
- Shopify backend for payment processing, inventory, fulfillment
- Custom-coded creator landing pages that feel branded to them
- White-label fulfillment through established 3PL network
- Shared customer service portal managed by company

### 6. Creator Contracts
**DECISION: 90-Day Performance Partnerships**
- Initial 90-day trial with specific performance benchmarks
- Automatic renewal if benchmarks met
- Creator exclusivity within their specific sub-niche only
- Clear IP ownership (company owns store, creator owns content)

### 7. When to Go Paid (Ads)
**DECISION: Validation Threshold Trigger**
**Triggers for paid amplification:**
- Product hits $2K organic revenue in first 30 days
- Conversion rate >2% on creator traffic
- Customer repeat purchase rate >15%
- When triggered: 70/30 budget split (organic/paid traffic)

### 8. Founder Role Division
**DECISION: Operations vs. Creator Relations Split**
Based on your background, recommend:
- **You**: Product sourcing, supplier relationships, fulfillment, unit economics
- **Partner**: Creator outreach, relationship management, content strategy, brand partnerships
