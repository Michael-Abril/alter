## MACROECONOMICS STUDY GUIDE - FINAL VERSION

### SECTION 1: MEASURING THE ECONOMY
**GDP Formula:** Y = C + I + G + NX
- **GDP Traps:** Owner-occupied housing (imputed rent), used goods (excluded), financial transactions (excluded)
- **GNP vs GDP:** GNP = GDP + Net Factor Payments from Abroad

**CPI 5-Step Process:**
1. Define basket of goods
2. Find prices in each year
3. Compute basket cost
4. Choose base year (=100)
5. Calculate index: (Current Cost/Base Cost) × 100

**CPI Flaws:** Quality bias, substitution bias, new product bias
**GDP Deflator vs CPI:** Deflator uses current basket, CPI uses fixed basket

### SECTION 2: LABOR MARKET
**Unemployment Rate:** (Unemployed/Labor Force) × 100
**Labor Force Participation:** (Labor Force/Adult Population) × 100
**Employment Rate:** (Employed/Adult Population) × 100

**Discouraged Worker Trap:** When workers stop looking → exit labor force → unemployment rate artificially falls

### SECTION 3: PRODUCTION & GROWTH
**Production Function:** Y = A·F(L,K,H,N)
- A = Technology, L = Labor, K = Capital, H = Human Capital, N = Natural Resources

### SECTION 4: FINANCIAL SYSTEM & POLICY
**"The Exam Shifts" - 3 Policy Shocks:**
1. **Monetary Shock:** Fed changes money supply → interest rates shift
2. **Fiscal Shock:** Government spending/tax changes → aggregate demand shifts  
3. **Supply Shock:** Oil prices, technology → aggregate supply shifts

**Financial System = Economic Circulatory System**
- **Bonds:** IOU, fixed return, safer
- **Stocks:** Ownership share, variable return, riskier
- **Municipal Bonds:** Tax-free (lower rate)
- **Corporate Bonds:** Taxable (higher rate to compensate)

**Key Formulas:**
- Inflation Rate: [(CPI₂ - CPI₁)/CPI₁] × 100
- Real Interest Rate: Nominal Rate - Inflation Rate
- Dollar Conversion: Amount₁ × (CPI₂/CPI₁)
