# 🗺️ FINAL 13-DAY PERU ITINERARY

## Days 1-2: Lima Arrival (2 nights)
**Stay:** Budget hostel in Miraflores ($20-30/night)
- **Day 1:** Arrive, recover, acclimatize
- **Day 2:** Food tour (ceviche, anticuchos), Miraflores cliffs walk
- **Evening Day 2:** Night bus to Paracas (3-4 hours, $8-15)

## Days 3-4: Paracas Coast (2 nights)
**Stay:** Paracas hostel ($10-15/night)
- **Day 3 AM:** Ballestas Islands boat tour ($15-25) - sea lions, penguins, dramatic formations
- **Day 3 PM:** Paracas National Reserve - red beaches, desert meets ocean
- **Day 4:** Travel day - bus back to Lima, fly to Puerto Maldonado

## Days 4-6: Amazon Jungle (3 days/2 nights)
**Stay:** Jungle lodge near Puerto Maldonado (included in tour $120-160)
- **Day 4 PM:** Arrive Puerto Maldonado, transfer to lodge
- **Day 5:** Full jungle day - canopy walk, caiman spotting, night hike
- **Day 6 AM:** Parrot clay lick, fly back to Lima, connect to Cusco

## Days 7-8: Cusco Acclimatization (2 nights)
**Stay:** Cusco hostel ($10-15/night)
- **Day 7:** Arrive, rest, gentle city walking, coca tea
- **Day 8:** San Pedro Market, Qorikancha temple, prep for altitude trek

## Days 9-10: Ausangate Trek (2 days/1 night)
**Stay:** High-altitude camping (included in tour $120-160)
- **Day 9:** Drive to trailhead, hike to base camp, colored mountains
- **Day 10:** Summit day, glacier views, hot springs, return Cusco

## Days 11-12: Machu Picchu (2 days/1 night)
**Stay:** Aguas Calientes hostel ($15-20/night)
- **Day 11:** Train to Aguas Calientes, explore town
- **Day 12 AM:** Machu Picchu sunrise ($120-160 total), return Cusco

## Day 13: Cusco → Lima → Home
- Morning flight Cusco-Lima ($40-50)
- International departure
