### Manufacturing Cost Flow Summary
```
Raw Materials → Work in Process → Finished Goods → Cost of Goods Sold
      ↓              ↓               ↓              ↓
   + Direct      + Direct       + COGM        = COGS
   Materials     Labor
```

### The Big 3 Product Cost Categories

**DIRECT MATERIALS (DM)**
- Raw materials you can trace directly to the product
- Examples: Wood for tables, fabric for shirts, steel for cars
- Formula: DM Used = Beginning RM + Purchases - Ending RM

**DIRECT LABOR (DL)**  
- Wages of workers who physically make the product
- Examples: Assembly line workers, seamstresses, welders
- NOT supervisors or maintenance crew

**MANUFACTURING OVERHEAD (MOH)**
- All other factory costs (indirect materials + indirect labor + other factory costs)
- Examples: Factory rent, utilities, depreciation on equipment, supervisor wages
- Gets allocated to products using a predetermined rate

### Classification Quick Tests

**Product vs Period Cost Test:**
- "Is this cost part of making the product?" → YES = Product Cost
- "Is this cost for selling/admin?" → YES = Period Cost

**Direct vs Indirect Test:**
- "Can I trace this cost to one specific unit?" → YES = Direct
- "Is this shared across multiple products?" → YES = Indirect (goes to MOH)

**Variable vs Fixed Test:**
- "Does this cost change with production volume?" → YES = Variable
- "Does this cost stay the same regardless of volume?" → YES = Fixed

### Common Exam Traps
- **Factory supervisor salary** = Product Cost (MOH), not Period Cost
- **Sales commission** = Period Cost, not Product Cost  
- **Indirect materials** (glue, screws) = Product Cost (MOH), not Direct Materials
- **Factory insurance** = Product Cost (MOH), not Period Cost
- **Office rent** = Period Cost, not Product Cost
