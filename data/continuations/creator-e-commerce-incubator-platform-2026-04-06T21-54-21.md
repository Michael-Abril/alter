# Creator E-commerce Incubator: Key Business Decisions Framework

Based on your progress, here are the 8 critical decisions with recommended choices to move forward:

## 1. Revenue Split Model
**Recommendation: Tiered Performance Split**
- New creators: 40% creator / 60% company
- Proven performers (>$5K monthly): 50/50 split
- Top performers (>$15K monthly): 60% creator / 40% company

This protects your margins early while incentivizing growth and retention.

## 2. Creator Transparency Level
**Recommendation: "Powered By" Model**
- Creators know they're part of a network but get individual branding
- Use tagline: "Powered by [YourCompany]" in footer
- Be upfront that products may be available through other creators
- Focus messaging on "you're our partner in this niche"

This balances honesty with creator buy-in.

## 3. Product Sourcing Strategy
**Recommendation: Hybrid Approach**
- Start with 3-5 proven dropship winners in health/fitness niche
- Graduate successful products to private label after $25K in sales
- Use creator feedback to guide private label decisions

## 4. Initial Niche Selection
**Recommendation: Fitness & Wellness (5K-50K followers)**
- High engagement rates and purchase intent
- Clear product-market fit opportunities
- Creators comfortable promoting lifestyle products
- Strong repeat purchase potential

## 5. Technical Infrastructure
**Recommendation: Shopify + Custom Creator Portal**
- Individual Shopify stores for each creator (better for brand trust)
- Custom dashboard for creators to track sales/earnings
- Shared product catalog on backend
- White-label fulfillment integration

## 6. Contract Structure
**Recommendation: 90-Day Performance Windows**
- 30-day trial period (no exclusivity requirements)
- 90-day performance review cycles
- Automatic renewal unless either party opts out
- Non-compete limited to identical product + identical niche

## 7. Monetization Timeline
**Recommendation: Revenue Share from Day 1**
- No upfront fees or monthly charges
- Pure performance-based model reduces creator risk
- Your investment is time + operational costs only
- Faster creator acquisition without financial barriers

## 8. Founder Role Division
**Recommendation: Specialization Split**
- Partner A: Creator relationships, content strategy, niche expansion
- Partner B: Operations, fulfillment, product sourcing, tech stack
- Shared: Strategic decisions, financial oversight, performance reviews
